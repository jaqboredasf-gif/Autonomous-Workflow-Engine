# Plain-language tests

One file per person. `<id>.md` is the field sheet you fill in; `npm run evidence -- --import`
turns it into `<id>.json`, which is what the readiness bands read.

**Empty today.** That is why `WHAT AWE IS` is `NOT_READY` and why it is the cheapest thing in the
whole project to move: five people, ten minutes each, no permission from anybody.

Two rules worth repeating:

- **Somebody inside the project is refused, not discounted.** They cannot be surprised by the
  sentence.
- **A verbatim echo is not a pass.** If they recited the sentence back rather than restating it,
  every concept is forced to GARBLED — "they said it back perfectly" is the most flattering
  possible result and the least informative.
